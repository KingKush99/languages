TARGET LANGUAGE STORY

Мальчик думал над трудной задачей для школы. Он долго
читал большую книгу, но никак не мог понять правильный ответ. Он был очень

весь смысл». Он начал снова.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The boy was thinking about a difficult task for
school. He read a big book for a long time, but could not understand the correct
answer. He was very upset by this.

Page 2:
His mother came close to him and quietly said: "Try
again from the first page. If you read slowly, you will surely understand the
whole meaning." He started again.
