TARGET LANGUAGE STORY

Мой дедушка подарил мне старую интересную книгу. В этой
книге было много красивых картинок с дикими животными со всего мира. Я сразу

и быстро.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
My grandfather gave me an interesting old book. In
this book, there were many beautiful pictures of wild animals from all over the
world. I loved this book immediately.

Page 2:
I read the book and learned to write new difficult
words by spelling them out. My mother sat next to me and helped me do it
correctly and quickly.
