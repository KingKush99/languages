ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
A very wide river flows near our village. To cross
to the other side of the land, we always had to walk along a long and difficult
road. It was inconvenient.

Page 2:
The people gathered together and decided to build a
new wooden bridge. Everyone worked hard with their hands, and the big bridge was
ready. Now we can walk easily.
