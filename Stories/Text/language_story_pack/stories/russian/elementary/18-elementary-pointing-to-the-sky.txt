ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
When we climbed to the top of the high mountain,
the blue sky seemed very close. A strong, cold wind was blowing, but the bright
light of the sun made the world around us beautiful.

Page 2:
Father pointed his hand to the sky and said: "Look
at those big birds. They are flying to distant, warm lands." We stood for a long
time and quietly followed them with our eyes.
