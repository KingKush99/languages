ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The girl tried to make a beautiful animal out of
paper. But her first attempt did not succeed, and the paper tore. She felt a
little sad and wanted to quit the work.

Page 2:
Mom came over and said: "Try again. Make your hand
movements slower." The girl took a clean sheet and was able to make an excellent
figure. She smiled.
