ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
We decided to make a plan and climb a very high
mountain. This mountain stood close to the clouds, and its peak required
strength and bold actions from us.

Page 2:
When we reached the top, a cold wind was blowing.
But the view of the earth from there was beautiful. Houses and rivers seemed
like small points. We were proud of our strength.
