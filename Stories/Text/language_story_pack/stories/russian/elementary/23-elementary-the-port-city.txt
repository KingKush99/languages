ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
My house stands very close to a big port. Every
morning, ships from all over the world come here. Many people step onto this
land, and it becomes noisy all around.

Page 2:
In the evening, my father and I go to the water. We
watch as the big ships head back out to sea. The light of the sun rests on the
waves, and this view is beautiful beyond any words.
