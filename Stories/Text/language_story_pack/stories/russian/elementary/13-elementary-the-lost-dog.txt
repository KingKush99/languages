ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
One day, a girl found a small dog in the forest.
The dog was lost and very cold. The girl quietly approached it, spoke in a sweet
voice, and calmed it down.

Page 2:
She brought the dog into her house and gave it some
warm food. The dog wagged its tail happily. Her mother and father agreed to keep
the dog, and all of them were very happy.
