Page 4:

оставил длинную линию в небесах. Все замолчали и смотрели на это чудо
природы.

Page 5:
Этот вечер помог детям полюбить науку. Некоторые из них
решили стать астрономами в будущем. Мария поняла, что ее работа имеет большую
ценность.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The astronomer Maria was waiting for the appearance
of a rare comet. This comet approached Earth once every hundred years. She
wanted to take high-quality pictures of its tail.

Page 2:
She organized a small evening school for children
on a hill outside the town. Many children came with their parents to look at the
sky.

Page 3:
Maria adjusted the large telescope and showed the
children constellations. She explained the laws of space to them in simple and
clear words. The children listened with interest.

Page 4:
Finally, the comet appeared in the dark sky. Its
bright light left a long line in the heavens. Everyone fell silent and looked at
this miracle of nature.

Page 5:
This evening helped the children fall in love with
science. Some of them decided to become astronomers in the future. Maria
realized that her work holds great value.
