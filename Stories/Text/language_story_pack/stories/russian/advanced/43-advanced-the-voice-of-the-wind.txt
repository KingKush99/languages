ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The scientist Kolya worked at a weather station
high in the mountains. He recorded wind and temperature indicators. Recently,
the weather in the mountains began to change rapidly.

Page 2:
Kolya realized that a dangerous storm was heading
towards the small village at the foot of the mountain. He needed to warn the
people to protect their homes from destruction.

Page 3:
He descended to the village and told the people
about the wind. Some did not believe his instruments, but the scientific data
convinced them to agree to a temporary evacuation.

Page 4:
At night, a strong hurricane began. The wind broke
trees near the houses. But thanks to Kolya's quick work, all the residents were
already safe in a stone shelter.

Page 5:
In the morning, the storm left. The village was
slightly damaged, but people's lives were saved. The residents thanked the young
scientist and understood the value of his work and science.
