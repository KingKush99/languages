Page 4:
Когда дождь прошел, Дмитрий пришел к месту, где текли две
реки. Там был красивый водопад. За водой он обнаружил скрытый вход в пещеру,
где лежал старый каменный ящик.

Page 5:
Дмитрий открыл ящик и нашел старые бумаги. В них была
записана история древних жителей этих мест и их полезные знания. Дмитрий
принес находку в университет и открыл забытую историю миру.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The young historian Dmitry was working in the
city's old library. Among the old books, he found a yellowed map. On it was
marked a place in the mountains that was not on modern maps.

Page 2:
Dmitry decided to investigate this mysterious
place. He studied the history of the area and prepared all the devices for a
long hike. He knew the path would be difficult, but his interest in history led
him.

Page 3:
The mountains were very high and steep. Along the
way, a heavy rain began, and it became cold. Dmitry was tired, but his desire to
find the truth helped him move further through the forest.

Page 4:
When the rain stopped, Dmitry came to the place
where two rivers flowed. There was a beautiful waterfall. Behind the water, he
discovered a hidden entrance to a cave where an old stone box lay.

Page 5:
Dmitry opened the box and found old papers. In them
was recorded the history of the ancient residents of these places and their
useful knowledge. Dmitry brought the find to the university and revealed the
forgotten history to the world.
