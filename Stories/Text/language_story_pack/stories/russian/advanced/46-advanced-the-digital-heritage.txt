ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The programmer Alexey found an old black-and-white
photo album in his grandmother's house. There were photographs of his family and
pictures of the town's life a hundred years ago.

Page 2:
Alexey decided to convert these pictures into a
digital format to preserve the family's history. He understood that paper could
deteriorate over time.

Page 3:
He used a scanner and wrote a special program on
his computer. The program removed dust from the images and made old faces clear.

Page 4:
Alexey uploaded the completed album to the internet
and sent the link to all relatives in other cities. Everyone was surprised and
glad to see their roots.

Page 5:
Thanks to modern technologies, the family's history
was saved for future children. Alexey realized that his knowledge could serve
the good goals of society.
