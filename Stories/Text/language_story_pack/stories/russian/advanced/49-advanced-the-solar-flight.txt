ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The engineer Yuri worked on creating a new vehicle.
It was a small drone that could fly solely on solar energy without gasoline.

Page 2:
The goal of the project was to deliver medical
devices and medicines to people in hard-to-reach mountain areas where roads were
destroyed by landslides.

Page 3:
On a sunny morning, Yuri took the vehicle out to a
wide field. The drone quietly rose into the air and flew towards the mountains
using only the power of light.

Page 4:
A strong wind arose in the mountains. But thanks to
the durable structure of the wing, the drone was able to maintain balance and
continued moving on course.

Page 5:
The drone successfully delivered the cargo to the
destination. Yuri was happy. His new technology proved its utility and helped
make a step toward saving lives.
