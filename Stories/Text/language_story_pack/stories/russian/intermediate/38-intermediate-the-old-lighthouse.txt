когда-то жили здесь и помогали кораблям в шторм.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
On a high cliff by the sea stood an old stone
lighthouse. It was no longer active now, because ships used modern systems. Lena
and her dad decided to go there for a walk on Sunday.

Page 2:
They opened the heavy wooden door and went inside
the lighthouse. It was cool inside, and a long stone staircase led upward. Lena
climbed carefully step by step, holding her dad's hand.

Page 3:
At the top on the balcony, a strong wind was
blowing. Before them opened a view of the vast blue sea with big waves. Lena
thought of the people who once lived here and helped ships during storms. ===
