ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In the spring, Dima was spending time at his
grandmother's dacha. His grandmother decided to teach him how to grow vegetables
with his own hands. They prepared the soil in the garden and planted several
tomato and cucumber seeds.

Page 2:
Every day after studying, Dima helped water the
garden beds. Two weeks later, the first green leaves appeared from the warm
soil. Dima was very happy to see the fruits of their common labor.

Page 3:
In the summer, they harvested many fresh tomatoes
and cucumbers. Grandmother and Dima made a delicious salad out of them for
dinner. The boy understood that food grown with one's own hands has the best
taste.
