ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Roma and Denis made a beautiful wooden boat during
their school craft lesson. They decided to launch it on a voyage along the clean
local river to see how it kept on the water.

Page 2:
The river current was calm, but along the boat's
path, there were fallen branches and large stones. The boys ran along the bank,
worrying whether their boat could bypass all these obstacles.

Page 3:
Downstream, Roma carefully retrieved the boat from
the water. It was completely undamaged. The boys returned home and recorded
their observations of the river current's speed in detail in their journal. ===
