ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In the summer, Misha went on a camping trip to the
mountains with his parents. At night by the tent, it was quiet and dark because
there were no city lights around. Misha went out into the fresh air and looked
up.

Page 2:
Misha's father took an interesting book about
constellations from his bag. He turned on a dim light and, looking at the book,
began to show his son the stars in the sky, explaining their names and ancient
stories.

Page 3:
Suddenly, a bright star quickly flew across the
dark sky, leaving a light trail behind it. It was a meteor. Misha managed to
quietly make a wish about his future. He would remember this evening forever.
