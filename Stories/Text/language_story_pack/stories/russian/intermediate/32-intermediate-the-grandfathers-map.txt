нашли настоящий привет из прошлого и почувствовали себя героями большой истории.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The boy Roma was looking for a book in his
grandfather's room. He opened an old desk drawer and found a paper at the very
bottom. It was an old map of the forest that his grandfather had drawn himself
when he was a small child.

Page 2:
Roma and his friend decided to go into the forest
using this map. They used old trees as landmarks and went deeper into the
forest. Soon they found an old stone well covered with green grass. Everything
was exactly as shown on the map.

Page 3:
They peered inside and saw a small plastic box.
Inside lay an old coin and a note with their grandfather's name. The boys had
found a real greeting from the past and felt like heroes of a grand story. ===
