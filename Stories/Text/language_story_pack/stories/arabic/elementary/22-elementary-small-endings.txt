ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Every thing in this world has an end. But any end
always means a beginning for something new and exciting. The young boy put all
his old toys in a large, closed box.

Page 2:
The boy has become older now and does not play with
those toys anymore. He must now read useful books and make new plans for his
life. He smiled and went on to build his future with confidence. === END OF
STORY 22 ===
