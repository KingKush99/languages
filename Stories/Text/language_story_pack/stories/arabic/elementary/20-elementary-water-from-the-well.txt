ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
This was a very hot day in summer. The air inside
the house was warm and heavy, and we needed cold, clean water. We decided to go
to the old well near the forest.

Page 2:
We used our hands to pull up the heavy bucket from
the well. The water was very cold and refreshing. We drank from it and felt
great happiness and energy, and our hot day became much better than before. ===
END OF STORY 20 ===
