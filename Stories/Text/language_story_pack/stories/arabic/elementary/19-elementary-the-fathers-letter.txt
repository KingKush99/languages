ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
My father traveled for work to a large port city
far away from our quiet house. One day, a written letter from him arrived. I
felt extreme joy and opened it immediately.

Page 2:
My father wrote in the letter about the beautiful
sea, wonderful places, and kind people there. He promised us that he would
return home very soon. My soul felt relieved, and the family was happy with this
news.
