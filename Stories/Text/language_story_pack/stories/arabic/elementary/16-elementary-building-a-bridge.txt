ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
A very wide river flows near our small village. To
go to the other side of the land, we always had to walk along a long and tiring
road. This was very inconvenient for us.

Page 2:
The villagers gathered and decided to build a new
wooden bridge. Everyone worked hard using their hands, and the big bridge became
ready. Now we can cross to the other side easily and very quickly. === END OF
STORY 16 ===
