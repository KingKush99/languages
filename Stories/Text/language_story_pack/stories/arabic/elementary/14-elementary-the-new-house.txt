الطيبة والمشجعة.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
We chose this quiet land to build our new house. A
clean river flows near us, and we hear the beautiful voices of birds every day.
Our new life begins in this beautiful place today.

Page 2:
My father and I started planting a small garden
near our new house. We planted colorful and beautiful flowers there. My mother
watched us from the window and helped us with her kind and encouraging words.
