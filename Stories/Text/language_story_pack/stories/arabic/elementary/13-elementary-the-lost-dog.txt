العائلة، وكان الجميع سعداء جداً.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
One day, a young girl found a lost dog in the
nearby forest. The dog was afraid and felt very cold. The girl approached it
gently and spoke to it in a quiet voice until it felt safe.

Page 2:
The girl took the dog to her house and gave it warm
food. The dog began to wag its tail happily. Her mother and father agreed to let
the dog stay with them as a family member, and everyone was very happy. === END
OF STORY 13 ===
