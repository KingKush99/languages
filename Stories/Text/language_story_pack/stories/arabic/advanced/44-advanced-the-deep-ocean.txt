Page 2:

بيديه بسرعة وهدوء.

Page 5:
عاد طارق إلى السطح وعرض صوره على علماء العالم. ساعد اكتشافه
الرائع الناس على فهم أعماق المحيط بشكل أفضل ومعرفة أهمية حماية طبيعته وبيئته
المائية.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The oceanographer Tariq built a new and advanced
diving device. He was planning to descend to the deepest point in the sea to
study the nature of unknown life on the bottom of the vast ocean.

Page 2:
At a depth of several thousand meters, there was
pitch and total darkness. But the powerful and modern lamps attached to the
device illuminated a mysterious world of sand, rocks, and strange plants.

Page 3:
Suddenly, Tariq saw a small and beautifully glowing
living creature. This was a exciting new species unknown to science before. He
took extremely clear pictures of this beautiful creature.

Page 4:
During the work, the instruments suddenly turned
off for one second. Tariq did not feel fear or panic. He knew the structure and
build of the device well, and fixed the electrical problem with his hands
quickly and calmly.

Page 5:
Tariq returned to the surface and displayed his
pictures to the world's scientists. His wonderful discovery helped people better
understand the ocean depths and know the importance of protecting its nature and
marine environment.
