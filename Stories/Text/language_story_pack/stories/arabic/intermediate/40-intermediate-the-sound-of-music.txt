ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Salma came with her kind mother to the town's old
concert hall. Today, there was a musical concert in which a famous violinist was
playing. The lights went out in the dark hall, and a warm candle was lit on the
stage to illuminate the place.

Page 2:
When the performance began, the violin melodies
sounded extremely warm and beautiful. The sounds of the music changed at times
slowly and quietly, and at other times rose quickly and strongly to capture
everyone's hearts. Salma felt great happiness and mental comfort with the
melodies.

Page 3:
After the concert, everyone clapped warmly and with
great strength. Salma felt highly inspired and had a genuine desire. When she
returned home, she decided to take out her old childhood toy piano to try
playing again and hearing its tones. A new dream was born in her today. === END
