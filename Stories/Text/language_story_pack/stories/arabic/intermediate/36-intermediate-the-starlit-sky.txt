ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In the summer, Karim went with his parents on a
camping trip in the high mountains. The night was very quiet and dark because
there were no city lights around them. Karim went out into the fresh air and
raised his eyes to watch the wonderful sky.

Page 2:
Karim's father took a wonderful book about
constellations from his bag. He turned on a dim flashlight and, looking at the
book, began to show his son the bright stars in the sky, explaining their names
and ancient stories.

Page 3:
Suddenly, a bright star flew quickly across the
dark sky, leaving a glowing and beautiful line behind it. This was a shooting
star. Karim managed to quickly make a wish in his heart about his future and
dreams. This night was a warm and beautiful experience for him. === END OF
STORY 36 ===
