ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
On a Saturday, Majid decided to clean the old attic
of his house. There were many old boxes and broken furniture. In the corner of
the room, he noticed a large, antique wooden clock. It looked as if it had not
worked for many years and was covered in dust.

Page 2:
Majid opened the clock's glass door and began
examining it from the inside. Behind the heavy pendulum, he found a small brass
key. He took it in his hand and tried inserting it into a small hole on the
side. The key fit perfectly and easily.

Page 3:
He turned the key slowly, and the clock began to
make a familiar ticking sound. The hands moved in a circle, and soon a beautiful
chime rang throughout the house. This sound was the same one his grandfather
loved listening to many years ago. Majid felt warm and joyful. === END OF
STORY 27 ===
