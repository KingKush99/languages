درجاً قديماً من أدراج المكتبة، وجد في قاعه ورقة مطوية. كانت خريطة قديمة للغابة

نظر الولدان داخل البئر ووجدا صندوقاً بلاستيكياً صغيراً. في
الداخل كانت عملة معدنية قديمة ورسالة صغيرة تحمل اسم جده عندما كان طفلاً. شعر
الولدان بسعادة غامرة وكأنهما في مغامرة تاريخية حقيقية.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Samir was looking for a book in his grandfather's
old study room. When he opened an old drawer of the desk, he found a folded
paper at the bottom. It was an old map of the nearby forest drawn by his
grandfather when he was a young child.

Page 2:
Samir and his friend decided to go to the forest
using this map. They used old, unique trees as landmarks and walked deeper into
the forest. Soon they found an old stone well covered with green grass, exactly
like on the map.

Page 3:
The two boys looked inside the well and found a
small plastic box. Inside was an old coin and a small note with their
grandfather's name when he was a child. The boys felt immense joy as if they
were in a real historical adventure.
