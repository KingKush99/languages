बना लिया। वह मुस्कुराई।

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
A little girl tried to fold a beautiful small
animal out of colored paper. But her first attempt did not succeed, and the
paper tore. She felt a bit sad.

Page 2:
Her mother came over and said: "Try again, and make
your hand movements a little slower." The girl took a clean new sheet of paper
and was finally able to fold an excellent shape. She smiled.
