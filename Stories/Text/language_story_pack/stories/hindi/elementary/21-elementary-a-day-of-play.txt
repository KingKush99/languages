ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Today is a school holiday. Small children gathered
in the open square near the houses. Everyone started playing lively. The weather
was great, and a comfortable breeze was blowing.

Page 2:
They played until evening. At the end of the day,
everyone sat in a big circle on the grass, telling each other interesting
stories they had read in books.
