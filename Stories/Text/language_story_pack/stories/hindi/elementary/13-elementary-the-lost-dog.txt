ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
One day, a little girl found a lost dog in the
nearby forest. The dog was very scared and felt very cold. The girl spoke to it
gently and made it feel safe.

Page 2:
She brought the dog back to her house and gave it
warm food. The dog happily wagged its tail. Her mother and father also agreed to
let it stay, and everyone was very happy.
