ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
My house is very close to a very large and busy
port. Every morning, big ships from all over the world arrive here, and many
people disembark onto this land; it is very lively.

Page 2:
In the evening, my father and I come to the water.
We quietly watch the big ships sail back to sea. The light of the setting sun
sprinkles on the waves, and this beautiful scenery cannot be described in words.
