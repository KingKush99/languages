ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Every thing in this world has its end. But an
ending always means the beginning of something new. The little boy put his old
toys into a large box.

Page 2:
He has grown up and doesn't play with those toys
anymore. Now he needs to read other more useful books and make new plans for
life himself. He smiled and walked confidently toward the future. === END OF
