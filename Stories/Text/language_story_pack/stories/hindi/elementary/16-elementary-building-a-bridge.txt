आसानी से नदी पार कर सकते हैं।

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
There is a very wide river near our village. In
order to go to the land on the other side, we always had to walk a long and
tiring road in the past. This made us feel very inconvenienced.

Page 2:
The villagers gathered together and decided to
build a new wooden bridge. Everyone worked hard with their hands, and the big
bridge was finally built. Now we can cross the river very easily and quickly.
