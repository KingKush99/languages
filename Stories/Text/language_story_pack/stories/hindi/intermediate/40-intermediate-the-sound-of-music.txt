ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Anjali came with her mom to the old city concert
hall. Today, an evening of violin music was taking place there. The lights went
out in the hall, and a warm lamp lit up on the stage, illuminating the musician.

Page 2:
The music sounded very beautiful. The sounds of the
music flowed slowly and quietly at times, and at others soared fast and loud.
Anjali listened to this melody, feeling how her mood changed along with the
sounds.

Page 3:
After the concert, everyone clapped loudly. Anjali
was highly inspired. At home, she decided to take out her small children's piano
and try to play songs on it again. A dream was born in her.
