ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In the spring, Vikas was spending time at his
grandmother's house. His grandmother decided to teach him how to grow vegetables
with his own hands. They prepared the soil in the garden and planted tomato and
cucumber seeds.

Page 2:
Every day after school, Vikas helped water the
garden beds. Two weeks later, the first green leaves appeared from the warm
soil. Vikas was very happy to see the fruits of their common labor.

Page 3:
In the summer, they harvested many fresh tomatoes
and cucumbers. Grandmother and Vikas made a delicious salad out of them for
dinner. The boy understood that food grown with one's own hands has the best
taste.
