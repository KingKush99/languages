ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The boy Rohan was looking for a book in his
grandfather's room. He opened an old desk drawer and found a paper at the very
bottom. It was an old map of the forest that his grandfather had drawn himself
when he was a small child.

Page 2:
Rohan and his friend decided to go into the forest
using this map. They used old trees as landmarks and went deeper. Soon they
found an old stone well covered with green grass, exactly like on the map.

Page 3:
They peered inside and saw a small plastic box.
Inside lay an old coin and a note with their grandfather's name. The boys had
found a real greeting from the past and felt very excited.
