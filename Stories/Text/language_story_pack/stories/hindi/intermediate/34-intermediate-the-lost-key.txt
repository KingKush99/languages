ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
On Sunday, Pooja was walking in the park with her
brother. She put her small desk key in her jacket pocket. But while playing on
the grass, the key fell out, and she became very upset.

Page 2:
Evening arrived, and it was quite dark. Pooja's
brother brought a small flashlight to help her. They walked together near the
large trees and shone light on the ground, carefully checking the grass.

Page 3:
Soon her brother noticed something shining under a
small grey stone. It was the lost key! Pooja was very happy and thanked her
brother. Returning home, she was able to open her desk drawer. === END OF
