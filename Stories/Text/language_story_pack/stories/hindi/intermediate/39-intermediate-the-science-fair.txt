ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Every autumn, a big science fair took place at
school. Rahul's group decided to prepare a project on clean energy. They built a
model that demonstrated the work of solar panels.

Page 2:
On the day of the fair, many teachers and parents
gathered in the hall. Many stopped at Rahul's project. The boy explained to the
guests simply and clearly how the light of the sun turns into electricity.

Page 3:
At the end of the day, the judges evaluated all
projects. The work of Rahul's group received a high rating and first place. The
children were very glad and understood how important teamwork is. === END OF
