ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Every Saturday Neha went to painting classes. Today
they needed to paint the view of the river from the large classroom window. She
prepared the canvas and put bright paints on the table. She really wanted to
start the work.

Page 2:
An hour later, the sky outside began slowly
changing its color to orange. A beautiful reflection of the sun appeared on the
river water. Neha mixed colors to convey this warm light on the canvas.

Page 3:
At the end of the lesson, all paintings were hung
on the wall. Although everyone painted the exact same view from the window, each
person ended up with their own unique painting. Neha understood that each person
sees the world in their own way.
