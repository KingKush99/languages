ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The gardener Geeta received a request to restore
the garden of an old monastery in the suburbs. This garden had been abandoned
many years ago and was overgrown with wild grass.

Page 2:
She drew a new plan for the garden. She decided to
place large grey stones near the water pool and make smooth sandy paths for
quiet walks for visitors.

Page 3:
Geeta planted soft green moss in the shade and
beautiful trees in the center of the garden. These trees were supposed to change
their color at different times of the year.

Page 4:
She wanted to create a place where every person
could forget about city problems, sit in silence, and think about their life and
soul.

Page 5:
The garden became a true place of peace for people.
They came to listen to the water and the wind. Geeta was glad she could return
beauty to the old land.
