Page 5:

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The architect Anil received an order to create a
new community center in the city. He decided to make the building as
eco-friendly and clean as possible.

Page 2:
He changed the structure of the roof to let more
sunlight inside the building. This helped reduce the use of electric lamps
during the day.

Page 3:
Anil planned to plant a green garden on the roof of
the new center. A rainwater collection system was supposed to water the plants
automatically without human involvement.

Page 4:
Builders started the work, using safe materials
made from recycled wood. City residents watched the creation of the new building
with interest.

Page 5:
The new center became the most beautiful place in
the city. People came there to rest under green trees. Anil proved the benefit
of green architecture.
