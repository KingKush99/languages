हमारा जीवन बहुत आसान हो गया है, लेकिन सुंदर प्रकृति को अक्सर नुकसान पहुँचता

फैक्ट्री के मालिक ने उसका सच्चा काम देखा और उसे एक आखिरी मौका

विज्ञान प्रकृति के साथ मिलकर काम कर सकता है।

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In today's modern society, science and technology
are developing rapidly. Our lives have become very easy, but beautiful nature
often suffers. In one small town, the river became dirty because of factories. A
young scientist, Amit, decided to fix this environmental problem.

Page 2:
Amit proposed building a new water purification
system. But the factory owners and some people opposed it. They believed it was
too expensive and would harm local work. Amit did not give up and kept up the
dialogue.

Page 3:
After long discussions, they decided to conduct the
first test. The scientists installed the equipment near the river. But the test
failed due to a machine breakdown. Dirty water flowed into the river again, and
people began to get angry.

Page 4:
Amit admitted his mistake and began looking for the
cause of the breakdown. He worked at night to repair the device and make it
safe. The factory owner saw his sincere work and decided to give him one last
chance.

Page 5:
The second test was successful. The water in the
river became completely clean. A few years later, fish returned to the river.
The town became an example of how science can work together in harmony with
nature.
