की जान बच गई। लोगों ने अमित को धन्यवाद दिया और विज्ञान की उपयोगिता को समझा।

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The scientist Amit worked at a weather station high
in the mountains. He recorded wind speed and temperature. Recently, the weather
in the mountains began to change rapidly.

Page 2:
Amit realized that a dangerous storm was heading
towards the small village at the foot of the mountain. He needed to warn the
people quickly to protect their homes.

Page 3:
He went down quickly to the village and told the
people about the wind. Some did not believe at first, but the scientific data
convinced them to agree to a temporary evacuation.

Page 4:
At night, a large storm raged. The wind broke trees
near the houses. But thanks to Amit's work, all the residents were already safe
inside a sturdy stone shelter.

Page 5:
In the morning, the storm left. The village was
slightly damaged, but people's lives were saved. The residents thanked Amit and
understood the utility of science.
