ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
A young historian, Dinesh, was working in the
city's old library. Among the old books, he found a yellow, old map. On it was
marked a place in the mountains that was not on modern maps.

Page 2:
Dinesh decided to investigate this mysterious
place. He studied the area's history and prepared all the devices for the long
hike. He knew the path would be difficult, but his interest in history guided
him.

Page 3:
The mountains were very high and steep. Along the
way, heavy rain began and it became cold. Dinesh was tired, but his desire to
find the truth helped him move further through the forest.

Page 4:
When the rain stopped, Dinesh came to the place
where two rivers flowed. There was a beautiful waterfall. Behind the water, he
found a hidden entrance to a cave where an old stone box was placed.

Page 5:
Dinesh opened the box and found old papers inside.
In them was recorded the history of the ancient people of these places and their
useful information. Dinesh showed this discovery at the university and revealed
the old history to the world.
