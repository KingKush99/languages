ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The scientist Rohan built a new underwater vehicle.
He planned to descend to the deepest point of the sea to study the unknown life
on the ocean floor.

Page 2:
At a depth of several thousand meters, there was
complete darkness. But the vehicle's powerful lamps illuminated an unknown world
of sand, stones, and strange plants.

Page 3:
Suddenly, Rohan saw a small glowing animal. It was
a new species unknown to science. He took many photos of this beautiful
creature.

Page 4:
During the operation, the instruments turned off
for a second. Rohan did not panic. He knew the vehicle's structure and quickly
fixed the electrical problem with his hands.

Page 5:
Rohan ascended and showed his pictures to the
world's scientists. His discovery helped people better understand the ocean
depths and know the importance of protecting its ecology.
