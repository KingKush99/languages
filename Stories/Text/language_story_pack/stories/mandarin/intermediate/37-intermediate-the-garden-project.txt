In spring, Xiao Hua spent a pleasant day in his
grandmother's big yard. His grandmother decided to teach him how to grow
vegetables with his own hands. The two tilled the soil in the garden and
carefully planted some tomato and cucumber seeds.

Page 2:
After school, Xiao Hua went to help his grandmother
every day and watered the plants with cool water. Two weeks later, small green
leaves emerged from the dark soil. Xiao Ming was very happy to see the fruits of
their combined effort.

Page 3:
In summer, they harvested many fresh tomatoes and
cucumbers. Grandmother and Xiao Hua made a delicious salad plate using these
home-grown vegetables. The boy felt that vegetables grown by oneself tasted
best.
