ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Every autumn, the school holds a large science
fair. Xiao Gang's group decided to make a project about clean energy. They built
a model showing how solar panels work.

Page 2:
On the day of the fair, many teachers and parents
gathered in the hall. Many people stopped before Xiao Gang's project. The boy
simply and clearly explained to the guests how sunlight is converted into
electrical energy.

Page 3:
At the end of the day, the judges evaluated all the
projects. Xiao Gang's group's work received an excellent rating and won first
place for its great idea. Everyone was happy for the teamwork. === END OF
