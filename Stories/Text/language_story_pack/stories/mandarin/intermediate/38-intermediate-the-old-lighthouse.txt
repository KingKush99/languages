ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
On a steep cliff by the sea stood an old stone
lighthouse. Since ships now use advanced equipment, no one is here anymore. On
Sunday, Xiao Li went for a walk there with her dad.

Page 2:
They opened the heavy old wooden door and walked
into the lighthouse. It was cool inside, and there was a long spiral stone
staircase. Xiao Li held her dad's hand, climbing up carefully step by step.

Page 3:
At the topmost balcony, the wind blew very
strongly. Before them lay the boundless blue sea and rolling waves. Xiao Li
thought of the people who used to live here helping big ships. === END OF
STORY 38 ===
