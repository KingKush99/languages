ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
On Sunday, Xiao Fang was playing with her younger
brother in the public park. She put her small desk key in her jacket pocket. But
while running and playing on the grass, the key fell out, which made her very
sad.

Page 2:
Night fell, and it became a bit dark in the park.
Her brother brought a small flashlight to help her. The two walked together near
the huge trees, pointing the light toward the ground, carefully checking every
patch of grass.

Page 3:
Soon her brother noticed a metallic shine under a
small grey stone. It was the lost key! Xiao Fang was very happy and thanked her
brother. Returning home, she successfully opened her small desk drawer. === END
