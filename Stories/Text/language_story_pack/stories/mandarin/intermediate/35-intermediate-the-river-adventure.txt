ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Xiao Jie and his friend made a beautiful small
wooden boat in their school crafts class. They planned to sail it in the clean
nearby river, observing its performance and balance on the water surface.

Page 2:
The river flowed smoothly, but there were some
branches and stones blocking the way. The two boys ran along the bank, feeling
nervous, worried that their small wooden boat might hit any obstacles in the
river.

Page 3:
At the downstream end of the river, Xiao Jie
carefully retrieved the boat, making sure not to get wet. The boat was
completely undamaged. The friends returned home and wrote down their
observations on the river flow's speed in detail in their notebook. === END OF
