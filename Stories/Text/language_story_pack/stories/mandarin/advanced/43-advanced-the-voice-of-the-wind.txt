Page 2:

Page 5:

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The weather observer Xiao Qiang worked at the
weather station at the top of the mountain. He recorded wind speed and
temperature data daily. Recently, the weather in the mountains began to change
rapidly.

Page 2:
Xiao Qiang realized from the readings that a
dangerous and strong storm was heading towards the small village at the foot of
the mountain. He needed to warn the residents quickly to protect their homes
from harm.

Page 3:
He went down quickly to the village and told
everyone about the coming wind. Some did not believe the instruments at first,
but the scientific data convinced them to agree to an immediate temporary
evacuation.

Page 4:
At night, a strong storm raged. The wind broke
large tree branches near the houses. But fortunately, thanks to Xiao Qiang's
quick and early work, all the residents were already safe inside a sturdy stone
shelter.

Page 5:
In the morning, the storm left. The village was
slightly damaged, but people's lives were safe and sound. The residents thanked
Xiao Qiang very much and understood the value of science, technology, and his
work.
