Story 42: The Ancient Map's Secret
Language: Chinese
Level: Advanced
Pages: 5
Assigned new-word slots: 686-720 (35 new words)

TARGET LANGUAGE STORY
----------------------
Page 1:
年轻的历史学者小刚在城市的老图书馆里工作。在整理旧书时，他发现了一张发黄的羊皮纸地图。上面标注了山里的一个神秘地点，而在现代地图上并没有这个地方。

Page 2:
小刚决定去探索这个神秘而未知的地方。他仔细研究了该地区的历史，并准备了所有适合长途探险的设备。他知道旅途会很艰难，但对历史的强烈兴趣引导着他。

Page 3:
大山非常高且十分陡峭。在路上突然下起了暴雨，天气也变得非常寒冷。小刚感到很疲惫，但他寻找历史真相的决心支持着他继续穿过茂密的森林。

Page 4:
雨停后，小刚来到了两条小河交汇的地方。那里有一个美丽而壮观的瀑布。在水幕后面，他发现了一个隐藏的洞穴入口，里面放着一个古老的石盒。

Page 5:
小刚打开石盒，找到了古老的文书。里面记录了这些地方古代居民的历史以及他们有用的草药知识。小刚把这个发现带回学校，将这段遗忘的历史展现给世界。

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The young historian Xiao Gang was working in the
city's old library. While organizing old books, he found a yellowed parchment
map. On it was marked a mysterious location in the mountains that did not appear
on modern maps today.

Page 2:
Xiao Gang decided to explore this mysterious and
unknown place. He studied the area's history well and prepared all the suitable
devices for his long adventure. He knew the path would be difficult, but his
strong interest in history guided him.

Page 3:
The mountains were very high and steep. On the way,
heavy rain began to fall, and it became extremely cold. Xiao Gang felt tired,
but his determination to find the historical truth supported him to continue
through the dense forest.

Page 4:
After the rain cleared, Xiao Gang reached the place
where two rivers intersect. There was a beautiful and spectacular waterfall.
Behind the water, he discovered a hidden entrance to a cave where an old stone
box lay.

Page 5:
Xiao Gang opened the box and found old papers. They
recorded the history of the ancient residents of these places and their useful
herbal knowledge. Xiao Gang brought this discovery back to the university and
revealed the forgotten history to the world.
