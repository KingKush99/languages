Story 17: The Picture Book
Language: Chinese
Level: Elementary
Pages: 2
Assigned new-word slots: 165-180 (16 new words)

TARGET LANGUAGE STORY
----------------------
Page 1:
我的祖父送给我一本古老而有趣的图画书。那本书里有许多来自世界各地的野生动物的美丽照片。我第一眼就爱上了它。

Page 2:
我一边读书，一边学习如何写出那些困难新词的拼写。我的母亲总是坐在我身边，亲切地帮助我正确、快速地学习。

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
My grandfather gave me an ancient and interesting
picture book. In that book, there were many beautiful photos of wild animals
from all over the world. I fell in love with it at first sight.

Page 2:
While reading, I learned how to write the spellings
of those difficult new words. My mother always sat next to me, kindly helping me
learn correctly and quickly.
