ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In the morning, Pavel is in the library. There is window and notebook nearby. Pavel reads slowly and notices a new word: quiet. The day feels good, and the language feels a little closer.

Page 2:
Pavel writes the word quiet in a notebook. Then Pavel thinks about reading. The place is quiet, so Pavel reads the sentence again and says it softly.
