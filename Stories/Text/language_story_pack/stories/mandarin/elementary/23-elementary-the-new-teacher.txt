Story 23: The New Teacher
Language: Chinese
Level: Elementary
Pages: 2
Assigned new-word slots: 261-275 (15 new words)

TARGET LANGUAGE STORY
----------------------
Page 1:
早上，玛莎去学校。旁边有老师和白板。玛莎慢慢地读，并注意到一个新词：“问题”。今天很好，语言也变得更近了。

Page 2:
玛莎把“问题”写在笔记本里。然后玛莎想一想“学习”这个主题。这里很安静，所以玛莎又读了一遍句子，并轻声说出来。

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In the morning, Masha is at school. There is teacher and board nearby. Masha reads slowly and notices a new word: question. The day feels good, and the language feels a little closer.

Page 2:
Masha writes the word question in a notebook. Then Masha thinks about learning. The place is quiet, so Masha reads the sentence again and says it softly.
