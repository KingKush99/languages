ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Words open new paths for us and completely change
our understanding of life. Every day, I study the meanings of new words and
write them in my notebook to remember them.

Page 2:
Reading books helps a person build their own unique
inner world. Through a large amount of reading, we begin to better understand
others and become gentler and kinder to everyone around us.
