Story 19: The Father's Letter
Language: Chinese
Level: Elementary
Pages: 2
Assigned new-word slots: 197-212 (16 new words)

TARGET LANGUAGE STORY
----------------------
Page 1:
我的父亲因为工作去了一个很远的大港口城市。有一天，家里收到了一封他写的信。我非常高兴，立刻打开了它。

Page 2:
父亲在信中写到了那里美丽的大海、奇妙的地方和善良的人们。他承诺很快就会回家。我的心立刻安定了下来。

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
My father went to a very far and large port city
because of work. One day, a letter he wrote was received at home. I was very
happy and opened it immediately.

Page 2:
Father wrote in the letter about the beautiful sea,
wonderful places, and kind people there. He promised to come home very soon. My
heart felt relieved immediately.
