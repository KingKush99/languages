ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
We chose this quiet land to build our new house.
There is a clean river nearby, and we can hear the beautiful songs of birds
every day. Our new life begins today.

Page 2:
My father and I started to build a small garden
near our new house. We planted many beautiful flowers there. My mother watched
us from the window and kindly helped us.
