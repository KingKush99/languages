ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
One day, a little girl found a lost dog in the
nearby forest. The dog was very scared and felt extremely cold. The girl spoke
to it gently and made it feel safe.

Page 2:
The girl took the dog home and gave it warm food.
The dog happily wagged its tail. Her mother and father also agreed to let it
stay, and everyone was very happy.
