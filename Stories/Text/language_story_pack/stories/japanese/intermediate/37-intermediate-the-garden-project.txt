ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Behind the school, there was a small unused space that had been empty for a long time. The grass had grown tall, and the soil had become hard. The class talked with the teacher and made a plan to turn the place into a vegetable and flower garden.

Page 2:
Yui became responsible for choosing seeds, while her friends took charge of watering and making signs. The first work was hard, and their hands and clothes became dirty with soil. Still, everyone cooperated while laughing and slowly shaped the garden.

Page 3:
A few weeks later, small green sprouts stood in rows. Eventually red tomatoes and bright flowers grew, and the back of the school became a cheerful place. Yui felt that things grown with their own hands had a special taste and meaning.
