ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
In a seaside town, there was an old lighthouse that had not been used for a long time. The wall color had faded, and dust covered the stairs. Even so, the people of the town wanted to protect the lighthouse as an important memory.

Page 2:
Kento helped clean with his friends. When they polished the windows, the distant sea looked blue. The adults repaired the broken lamp, and the children arranged old photographs to make a small display.

Page 3:
In the evening, the lighthouse light reached out toward the sea for the first time in a long while. The townspeople clapped, and Kento felt moved. He learned that even an old building could have a new role if everyone worked together.
