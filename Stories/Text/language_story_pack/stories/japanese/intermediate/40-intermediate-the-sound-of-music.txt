あやは母と一緒に、町の古い音楽室を訪れました。その日は子供たちのための小さな演奏会がありました。部屋の明かりが少し暗くなると、舞台の上でバイオリンが静かに光りました。

演奏が始まると、音はゆっくり流れたり、急に高く跳ねたりしました。あやは目を閉じて、音の中に雨、風、明るい朝の景色を感じました。言葉がなくても、音楽は気持ちを伝えられるのだと思いました。

Page 3:

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Aya visited the town's old music room with her mother. That day there was a small concert for children. When the room lights became a little dim, a violin quietly shone on the stage.

Page 2:
When the performance began, the sounds flowed slowly and sometimes jumped suddenly high. Aya closed her eyes and felt rain, wind, and a bright morning scene inside the music. She thought that even without words, music could communicate feelings.

Page 3:
After the concert, everyone clapped loudly. When she returned home, Aya opened the small piano she had used long ago. She was not skilled yet, but a new dream was born: she wanted to practice again.
