Page 4:

この体験は、多くの子どもたちに科学への情熱と宇宙への憧れを与えました。サクラは、未来の科学者を育てる価値を実感しました。

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Sakura, an astronomer, was preparing to observe a
rare comet that approaches Earth only once in several decades.

Page 2:
She planned a small observation meeting on the
mountain for local children. Many families gathered.

Page 3:
She adjusted the large telescope and let the
children peer at the beautiful starlit sky. "The universe is so wide," the
children said.

Page 4:
Finally, the comet appeared in the night sky. With
its beautiful light trailing a long tail, everyone lost their words and watched
quietly.

Page 5:
This experience gave many children a passion for
science and a longing for space. Sakura realized the value of nurturing future
scientists.
