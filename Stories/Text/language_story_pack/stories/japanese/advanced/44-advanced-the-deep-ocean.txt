ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Ken, an oceanographer, made a plan to investigate
the deepest parts of the sea using a new deep-sea explorer.

Page 2:
The world thousands of meters deep was complete
darkness. However, the strong light of the explorer illuminated the beautiful,
unknown seafloor.

Page 3:
Ken discovered a beautiful, glowing new creature.
He was convinced that it would be an important discovery in the history of
science.

Page 4:
During the investigation, the communication system
temporarily malfunctioned. The team on the ground was worried, but Ken calmly
repaired the machine's structure.

Page 5:
Ken returned safely and showed the beauty of the
deep sea to people around the world through pictures. His research became a
great foundation for protecting the marine environment.
