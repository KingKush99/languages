ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Riku, a scientist, was studying the movement of the
wind at a weather station on the mountain. Recently, the mountain weather was
changing rapidly.

Page 2:
Riku learned that a strong storm was heading
towards the village. He was developing a new warning system to protect the
village.

Page 3:
He went down to the village and explained the
danger of the storm to the people. At first, some did not believe him, but they
understood Riku's scientific data and agreed to evacuate.

Page 4:
On the night of the storm, strong wind and rain hit
the village. However, thanks to Riku's system, all the people had evacuated to a
safe place.

Page 5:
The next morning, the storm left. The houses were
slightly damaged, but people's lives were saved. The villagers deeply thanked
Riku and realized the value of science and technology.
