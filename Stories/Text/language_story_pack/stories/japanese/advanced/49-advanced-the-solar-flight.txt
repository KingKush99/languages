ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Yuki, an engineer, was developing a new small
unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) that can fly solely on solar energy.

Page 2:
The purpose of this drone was to quickly deliver
medicines to distant disaster-hit areas where roads were broken. It was an
important technology for saving lives.

Page 3:
On a sunny morning, the first test flight was
conducted in a wide test area. The drone quietly climbed into the sky.

Page 4:
A strong wind temporarily blew in the upper sky,
but thanks to the stable structure designed by Yuki, the drone continued to fly
without losing balance.

Page 5:
The test flight was brilliantly successful, and it
was able to deliver the package to the target location accurately. Yuki was
convinced that he contributed to future rescue systems.
