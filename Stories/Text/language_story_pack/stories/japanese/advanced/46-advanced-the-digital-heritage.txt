ENGLISH TRANSLATION

Page 1:
Takuya, a programmer who writes source code, found
many faded black-and-white photo albums at his grandmother's house.

family history. Takuya made a plan to digitalize and save them.

Page 3:

Page 4:
He shared the completed digital album with
relatives all over the world using the internet. The relatives were very happy.

Page 5:
Through digital technology, the family history was
safely passed down to future generations. Takuya reaffirmed the true value of
technology.
