ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
Haru, a garden designer, received a request to
restore a traditional Japanese garden at an old temple in town.

Page 2:
She carefully planned the structure of the garden.
She arranged large stones and made small waves in the sand to express a natural
river.

Page 3:
She planted beautiful moss in the shade and planted
a beautiful cherry blossom tree in the center of the garden, which shows
different colors each season.

Page 4:
She worked hard to create a calm space where people
visiting the temple could quietly face themselves.

Page 5:
The restored garden became a place that gave many
people peace of mind. Haru felt the value of connecting traditional beauty to
the future.
