ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
The girl was trying to fold a paper into the shape
of a small animal. However, her first attempt failed, and the paper tore. She
became a little sad.

Page 2:
Her mother told her, "Try again. Next time, it's
better to fold it more slowly." Using a new sheet of paper, she was finally able
to fold a beautiful shape.
