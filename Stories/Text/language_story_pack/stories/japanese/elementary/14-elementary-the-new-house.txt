ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
We chose this quiet land to build a new house.
There is a river nearby, and we can hear birds singing every day. A new life
begins here starting today.

Page 2:
My father and I began to build a small garden near
the house. We planted beautiful flowers there. Watching from the window, my
mother helped us very kindly.
