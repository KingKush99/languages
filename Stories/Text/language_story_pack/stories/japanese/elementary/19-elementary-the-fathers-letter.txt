ENGLISH TRANSLATION
-------------------
Page 1:
My father went to a distant, large port town for
work. One day, a letter from my father arrived at the house. I was happy and
opened the envelope immediately.

Page 2:
Written in the letter were details about the
beautiful scenery of that land and meetings with new people. My father wrote, "I
will return again soon." I felt relieved.
